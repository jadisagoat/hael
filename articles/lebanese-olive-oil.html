Lebanese Extra Virgin Olive Oil from Hasbaya

HAEL is a family olive oil from Mimess, a village in the Hasbaya district of South Lebanon, near the foothills of Mount Hermon. It is named after the grandfather and carried on by the grandson.

Where it comes from

Hasbaya is one of Lebanon's best-known olive-growing areas. Olive trees have shaped its hillsides and its kitchens for generations. HAEL comes from the family's own groves in Mimess.

How it is made

The olives are handpicked in season and cold-pressed: washed, crushed and separated mechanically, with no additives and no preservatives. Nothing is blended in.

What "extra virgin" means in numbers

Extra virgin is a measured grade, not a marketing word. A chemical analysis of a HAEL sample in October 2026 classified it as extra virgin olive oil:

Olive paste with zaatar

Alongside the oil, HAEL makes an olive paste with zaatar: olives, zaatar, garlic and extra virgin olive oil, ready to spread on warm bread or stir into labneh.
